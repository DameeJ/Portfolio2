Overview

About this project

This dashboard analyzes Airbnb listing data to understand how price relates to listing size and location — looking at average price by bedroom count, price variation across zip codes, and how total revenue trended over the course of the year.

Key Findings

What the data showed

Price climbs sharply with bedroom count — but most listings are small

Average nightly price rose steadily from $96.20 for 1-bedroom listings to $584.80 for 6-bedroom listings — over a 6x increase. But listing supply runs in the opposite direction: 1-bedroom units made up the overwhelming majority of the market (1,811 listings), while 6-bedroom listings were rare (just 5).

Price varied significantly by zip code

Zip code 98101 had the highest average price at $200, followed by 98102 at $170 — both well above the broader market range, likely reflecting prime downtown or close-in neighborhoods.

Revenue grew sharply early in the year, then leveled off

Weekly revenue climbed quickly through the first few months of the year before settling into a fluctuating plateau for the remainder of the period.
